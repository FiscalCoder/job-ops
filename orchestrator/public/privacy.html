Job Ops Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

Job Ops is a personal job-search tool run by Hueint for a small number of invited users. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

If you connect Gmail, Job Ops reads email headers (sender, subject and date) and, for job-related messages, the message content. It uses them to find replies from employers and link them to the jobs you applied for.

Access is read-only. Job Ops never sends, deletes or changes your email.

How the data is used

Job-related messages are classified, sometimes with an AI model, and stored on Hueint's server so they show up next to the matching job. Your data is not sold, not shared with advertisers, and not used for anything else.

Google user data

Job Ops's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can disconnect Gmail in Job Ops at any time, or remove its access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To have stored messages deleted, contact us at the address below.
